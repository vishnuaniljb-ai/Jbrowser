# Privacy Policy for JBrowser

**Last updated:** August 2026

Welcome to **JBrowser**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how JBrowser handles user data.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
JBrowser is built with a privacy-first architecture:
* **Zero Tracking & Browsing History:** We do not collect, store, or monitor your search history, visited websites, or downloaded content.
* **No Personal Identity Data:** We do not require registration, personal accounts, or email addresses to use the browser.

## 2. Local Device Storage & Features
All browser functions operate locally on your device:
* **Private Vault & Biometrics:** Saved credentials and vault files are encrypted using hardware-backed AES-256 encryption directly on your device. We cannot access your vault or biometric authentication data.
* **Ad & Popup Blocker:** Blocking logic executes on-device without reporting visited domains to external servers.
* **Downloads:** Downloaded files are stored strictly on your local device storage.

## 3. VPN, Proxy & Network Services
* If you enable the built-in VPN, DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH), or proxy features, network traffic is routed through encrypted tunnels to secure your connection.
* We do not log traffic destinations, DNS requests, or browsing payloads.

## 4. App Permissions
JBrowser requests only essential Android permissions:
* **Storage:** To save downloaded media and files to your device.
* **Biometrics / Fingerprint:** To unlock the Private Vault locally.
* **Network / Internet:** To load webpages and connect to the web.

## 5. Third-Party Links & Content
JBrowser renders third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, tracking scripts, or content of external sites you choose to visit.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official repository or support channels.
